# Fixna LocalBoost PRD

## Vision
Give local businesses a simple way to plan, launch and measure geographically targeted
promotions across advertising channels.

## Users
Business owners, marketing users, agencies and platform administrators.

## MVP
Authentication, multi-tenancy, business/location management, campaign wizard,
geo/audience targeting, budget allocation, AI recommendations, creative drafts,
mock platform execution, analytics, leads, admin foundation and audit.

## Non-goals
Autonomous ad spending, full CRM, advanced attribution, mobile apps, payment
provider integration, production provider credentials.

## Core journey
User registers -> tenant -> business -> campaign -> offer -> geography -> audience ->
budget -> AI recommendation -> creatives -> review -> approval -> mock launch ->
metrics/leads.

## Success metrics
Activation, campaigns created, approved/launchable campaigns, lead conversion,
cost-per-lead visibility, tenant retention and AI recommendation acceptance.
